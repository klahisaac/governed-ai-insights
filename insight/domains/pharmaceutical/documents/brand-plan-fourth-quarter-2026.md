Priorities for October to December 2026:

1. Grow switches from omalizumab among patients with chronic spontaneous urticaria
   who remain symptomatic. Allergy and immunology practices are the first focus.
2. Reach a run rate of 100 new-to-brand patients per week nationally by 2026-12-31.
3. Keep the share of new patients who are new to advanced therapy above 35 percent,
   so the brand is not only a switch option.

Field resources stay as they are through the quarter. The plan will be reviewed on
2026-11-15 against claims data through the end of October.
