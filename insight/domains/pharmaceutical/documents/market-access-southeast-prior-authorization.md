Gulf Coast Health Plan, the largest commercial plan in the Southeast region, adds a
prior authorization requirement for Zelvora effective 2026-09-14.

What changes for offices:
- The plan now requires documented use of one other advanced therapy before approval.
- Typical turnaround for a complete request is 5 to 10 business days.
- Patients already on Zelvora are not affected until their next renewal.

The hub team can help offices prepare requests. Field representatives should not fill
in or submit forms on behalf of offices.
