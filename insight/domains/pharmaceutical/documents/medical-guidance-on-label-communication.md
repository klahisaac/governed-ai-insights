All promotional discussion must stay consistent with the approved label.

- Do not make comparative efficacy or safety claims against omalizumab, dupilumab or
  any other therapy. No head-to-head study exists.
- Questions outside the label go to Medical Information, not to the field.
- Report any adverse event you hear about within 24 hours, even if the patient's
  doctor has already reported it.
